## Overall verdict (original)

The project is in strong shape for a small, focused repo. Weaknesses concentrated in:

1. Error handling that can hide failures in the data pipeline  
2. Operational gaps (stale KEV, no refresh automation, unpinned deps)  
3. Frontend polish (no tests, thin a11y, UX dead-ends)

**Owner acceptance:** Verdict still accurate as of re-audit. All three weakness areas remediated or consciously residualled below.
